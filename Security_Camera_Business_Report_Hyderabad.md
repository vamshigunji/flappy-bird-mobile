## 2. Manufacturing & Assembly Workflow
Establishing a unit involves several high-precision stages, particularly when dealing with optical sensors.

### 2.1 Component Sourcing (Import Focus)
*   **Core Electronics:** Image Sensors (Sony/OmniVision), SoCs (Ambarella/Fullhan), Flash Memory, and DDR RAM.
*   **Optics:** Fixed or Varifocal lenses.
*   **Mechanicals:** Die-cast aluminum housings and IR cut filters.
*   **HSN Code:** 8525 89 00 (CCTV Cameras).

### 2.2 Production Line Stages
1.  **SMT Line (Surface Mount Technology):** Automated mounting of components onto PCBs.
2.  **Cleanroom Assembly (Class 10,000):** Essential for sensor-to-lens mounting to prevent dust artifacts.
3.  **Final Assembly:** Integrating PCBA, IR boards, and housings.
4.  **Testing & QC:** Waterproof testing (IP66/67) and 24-48 hour stress/ageing tests.

## 4. Software & Security Requirements
Per the **MeitY Gazette Notification (2024)**, hardware sold in India must meet stringent cybersecurity standards:
*   **No Default Passwords:** Mandatory user-defined credentials on first boot.

*   **Data Residency:** Preferential local cloud hosting for Indian consumers.
